Our Story

From Khayelitsha to your home — craftsmanship with heart.

How It Started

Founded in the heart of Khayelitsha, Sipho's Custom Carpentry was born from a young man's determination to build a future for himself. After studying carpentry, he started from the ground up, using his skills to create beautiful, functional pieces for his local community.

What began as small projects has grown into a trusted local business, specializing in custom kitchen cabinets, wardrobes, and TV stands. We believe that quality craftsmanship should be accessible to everyone.

Meet the Founder

Sipho is a passionate artisan who believes in paying attention to the smallest details. He oversees every project personally, ensuring each cabinet, wardrobe, or TV stand fits perfectly into the client's space.

Our Mission

To provide high-quality, durable, and affordable custom woodwork to homes across Khayelitsha and greater Cape Town.

Our Vision

To become a leading name in custom carpentry, recognised for our dedication to our clients, our craftsmanship, and our roots.
