Material Options Explained

Melamine is a cost-effective option for kitchen cabinets and wardrobes. It is easy to clean, resists moisture, and comes in a wide range of colours. Ideal for rental properties and budget-conscious renovations.

Solid wood offers natural beauty, durability, and long-term value. Oak and pine are popular choices for custom kitchen cabinets and TV stands. Best for homeowners wanting a premium finish.
